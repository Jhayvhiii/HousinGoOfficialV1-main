About HousinGo

A centralized rental property finder platform built for Barangay East Rembo, Taguig City.

🏘️

Our Mission

Connecting local tenants and landlords

HousinGo provides an accessible, digital platform for house seekers and landlords in Brgy. East Rembo, Taguig City. Our goal is to make property searching efficient, transparent, and community-focused.

By digitizing the rental process, we eliminate the hassle of traditional house hunting and make it easier for everyone in the community to find their ideal match.

✨

Why Choose HousinGo?

Built for transparency and convenience

  • Community-Focused: Tailored for Brgy. East Rembo & nearby Taguig areas.
  • Interactive GPS Map: Easily locate rentals and view distance from your location.
  • Transparent Details: Clear monthly rent, utilities, advance payments, and rules.
  • Verified Landlords: Admin-verified accounts and direct messaging.
  • Free Access: Browse properties and connect with landlords at no cost.
🔍

For House Seekers

Students, workers, and families looking for affordable, safe, and convenient rooms, bed spacers, studios, and apartments in East Rembo.

🔑

For Landlords

Property owners who want to showcase their listings with photos and GPS pins, manage applications, and communicate with tenants in one place.
