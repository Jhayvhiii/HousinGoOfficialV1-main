Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How to Register and Log In

▼

To start using HousinGo, you need to create an account first.

📝 Steps to Register:

  • Click on the "Register" button on the top menu.
  • Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Mobile Number, and Password.
  • Click "Create Account."
  • You will receive a confirmation email — open it and verify your account.

🔐 To Log In:

  • Click "Log In" on the menu.
  • Enter your registered email and password.
  • You can also choose "Continue with Facebook" or "Continue with Google" for faster access.

💡 Tip: Always keep your password private and never share it with anyone.

2. How to Post a Property

▼

If you are a landlord or property owner, you can easily post your available space.

📋 Steps to Post:

  • Log in to your Landlord Account.
  • Click "Post Property" on your dashboard.
  • Fill in all details such as:
    • Property Type (Bed Spacer, Room, Apartment, Single Family, etc.)
    • Location and Address
    • Monthly Rent Price
    • Photos of the property
    • Description (amenities, rules, nearby places, etc.)
  • Click "Submit" to post your property.
  • Wait for admin approval before it becomes visible to tenants.

💡 Tip: Upload clear and real photos to attract more tenants.

3. How to Contact a Landlord

▼

If you're a tenant interested in a property:

  • Go to the property page you like.
  • Click the "Contact" button.
  • You can send a message through the system or see the contact details (if available).
  • Wait for the landlord to respond via your registered email or in your account inbox.

💡 Tip: Be polite and include important details like your move-in date or budget.

4. How to Report a Problem

▼

If you encounter an issue — such as a fake listing, duplicate property, or technical error:

🚨 Steps:

  • Click "Contact Us" on the main menu.
  • Fill out the form:
    • Name
    • Email
    • Subject (e.g., "Fake Listing Report")
    • Message (describe the issue clearly)
  • Click "Send."
  • The admin team will review your report and contact you if needed.

You can also email us directly at: support@housingo.com
