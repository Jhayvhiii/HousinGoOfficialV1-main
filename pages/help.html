Frequently Asked Questions

Quick guides for Tenants and Landlords using HousinGo.

1. How to Register and Log In

▼

Open the ☰ Menu on the upper-left and select Register. Choose whether you are joining as a Tenant or Landlord, fill in your details, and click Create Account.

Once registered, use your Email and Password on the Log In page to access your personalized dashboard.

2. How to Post a Property (Landlords)

▼

Log in to your verified Landlord Account and click ＋ Add New Property in the upper-right of your dashboard. Upload photos, pin the GPS location on the map, add utility/advance payment details, and publish!

Note: New landlord accounts require Admin verification before publishing listings.

3. How to Apply & Contact a Landlord

▼

Click View Details on any listing (or click its price pin on the GPS map). From there, tenants can click 📝 Apply Now, 💬 Message Landlord, or open the landlord's Facebook/Messenger link directly.

4. How to Report a Listing or Problem

▼

Click the 🚨 Report button on any property listing or 🚨 File Complaint inside your Tenant Dashboard. Our Admin team reviews all reports and updates their status.
